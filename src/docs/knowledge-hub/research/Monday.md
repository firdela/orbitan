# Monday.com — Competitor Research

## Overview

Monday.com is a work management platform for teams. It provides boards, dashboards, and automations for project management, task tracking, and team collaboration. It targets SMBs to mid-market.

## Strengths

- **Simplicity** — Intuitive, easy-to-use interface with no training required
- **Visual workflows** — Color-coded boards and visual dashboards
- **Flexibility** — Customisable boards for any workflow
- **SMB-friendly** — Accessible pricing and onboarding
- **Integrations** — Broad integration marketplace

## Weaknesses

- **Not an OS** — Task management, not a complete business operating system
- **No industry packs** — Generic work management
- **No AI** — Limited AI capabilities (some automations)
- **No governance** — No policy-as-code
- **No financial operations** — No invoicing, reconciliation, or ERP integration
- **No workforce management** — No clock-in/out, shifts, payroll
- **No self-improvement** — Static analytics

## Architecture

- Multi-tenant SaaS
- Board-based data model
- Visual workflow builder
- REST APIs for integration

## AI

- Limited — Some AI automations and suggestions
- No kill switch
- No standalone AI product

## Lessons for Orbit

1. **Simplicity wins** — Monday.com's success proves that simplicity and ease-of-use matter. Orbitan must remain accessible to HBBs and first-time technology users.
2. **Visual workflows** — Visual workflow builders are popular. Orbit Flow could benefit from a visual builder (future).
3. **Work management is not an OS** — Monday.com is task management, not a complete business OS. OrbitanOS is a Workforce Operating System — a higher-value category.
4. **Accessibility** — Monday.com's no-training-required approach validates the Golden UI/UX Standard's focus on intuitive design.

## Orbit Opportunities

- **Complete OS** — OrbitanOS is a full Workforce OS, not just work management
- **Industry packs** — Orbitan is industry-specific; Monday is generic
- **Financial operations** — Orbitan has invoicing, reconciliation, ERP; Monday has none
- **Workforce management** — Orbitan has clock-in/out, shifts, payroll; Monday has none
- **AI intelligence** — Orbit Nexus; Monday has limited AI
- **Governance** — Orbit Shield; Monday has none
