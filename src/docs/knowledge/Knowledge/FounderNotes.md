## Strategic Principles

### 1. Platform Business, Not Internal Tool
Orbitan must always be treated as a platform business, not a tenant-specific internal tool. The current pilot tenants are validation environments, not the final limitation of the platform.

### 2. Tenants Consume Orbitan. Tenants Do Not Define Orbitan.
The platform vision, governance, and strategic direction remain under Product Owner authority.

### 3. Scalability First
Before implementing any feature, ask: "Will this still work when Orbit serves thousands of organisations, millions of records, multiple industries, multiple countries, and future marketplace ecosystems?" If the answer is no, redesign the solution.

### 4. Build Less. Validate More.
MVP discipline over feature bloat. Features explicitly out of MVP scope are deferred correctly.

### 5. No Fictional Data
Never create fake companies, brands, outlets, or employees. Use "Pending Setup" / "Coming Soon" placeholders. The platform must be built on real-world structures.

## Future Ownership Considerations

The final ownership structure for Orbitan has not yet been determined. Possible corporate arrangements:
- Orbitan Pte Ltd
- Orbitan LLC, with future conversion to Pte Ltd
- Fetch Innovation Pte Ltd
- A newly established Holding Company
- Joint Founder Ownership Structure

No final decision has been made. Therefore, OrbitanOS must remain architecturally independent from any future ownership arrangement. The platform must be capable of supporting both internal pilot businesses and external paying customers regardless of how the eventual corporate structure evolves.
