# Orbitan Marketing Principles

## Purpose

Defines marketing principles, messaging framework, brand voice, and content strategy.

## Marketing Principles

1. **Platform, not tool** — Position Orbitan as an operating system, not a point solution
2. **Two products** — OrbitanOS (workforce) and Orbit Nexus (AI) are separately marketable
3. **Industry-first** — Industry Packs demonstrate vertical understanding
4. **Enterprise-grade from day one** — Compliance, governance, audit trails as proof points
5. **Self-improving** — Orbit Evolution as a differentiator
6. **No fictional data** — Marketing uses real pilot tenant success stories, not invented metrics

## Brand Voice

- **Direct and practical** — No over-explanation
- **Professional but approachable** — Enterprise-grade without being cold
- **Confident** — The platform speaks with authority
- **Honest** — "Early Access" and "Pilot Programme" are stated openly

## Messaging Framework

### Primary Message
> Run Your Business. Connect Everything.

### Secondary Message
> One ecosystem. Two products. OrbitanOS runs your workforce and operations. Orbit Nexus powers it with AI.

### Proof Points
- Registry-Driven Architecture (add an industry without code changes)
- Wallet-Native Ledger (works without ERP)
- Orbit Shield (policy-as-code governance)
- Orbit Evolution (self-improving platform)
- Enterprise compliance readiness (SOC 2, ISO 27001, Vanta)

## Content Strategy

- **Landing page** — Dual product sections, pricing, industry packs, trust/shield
- **Auth Gateway** — Intelligent entry hub routing users to the right flow
- **Pricing** — Dynamic from config, 5 plans (Free → Enterprise)
- **Industry Packs** — F&B, Retail, Recycling featured; more coming
- **Trust** — Orbit Shield section with security, governance, audit trail

## Do's

- Use "OrbitanOS" for the product, "Orbitan" for the company
- Use "Orbit" prefix for platform services (Orbit Shield, Orbit Nexus, etc.)
- Show real pilot tenant industries (F&B, Recycling, Retail, HBB)
- Mention "Early Access — Pilot Programme Active" honestly

## Don'ts

- Don't create fictional companies, brands, or success metrics
- Don't prefix every service with "Orbitan" (loses impact through repetition)
- Don't hide pricing behind "Contact Us" (except Enterprise)
- Don't over-promise AI capabilities (AI is enhancement, not replacement)
