# Orbitan Go-To-Market Strategy

## Purpose

Defines the market entry strategy, channels, positioning, and launch plan.

## Market Entry Strategy

### Phase 1 — Pilot Validation (Current)
- 4 pilot tenants validate architecture, workflows, and modules
- Real-world operational data drives product refinement
- Feedback loop: IssueLog → nexusFeedbackAnalyst → product backlog

### Phase 2 — Self-Serve Public Launch
- Public landing page with marketing, pricing, and onboarding
- Auth Gateway routes users: Login / Join / Request Access / Create Organisation
- Self-serve onboarding wizard with AI-powered diagnostic (Find My Solution)
- Stripe checkout for paid plans

### Phase 3 — Enterprise Sales
- Direct sales motion for Enterprise tier
- Compliance certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001) as proof points
- White-label deployments
- Dedicated infrastructure and SLA

## Channels

| Channel | Phase | Description |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| Pilot Programme | Phase 1 | Taqueria, Renewed Resources, Renewed Fashion, Izaliqa Bakes |
| Public Website | Phase 2 | Landing page → Auth Gateway → Onboarding |
| Stripe Checkout | Phase 2 | Self-serve subscription for Growth and Business plans |
| Direct Sales | Phase 3 | Enterprise tier, custom pricing |
| Marketplace | Future | Third-party modules and industry packs |

## Positioning

> **Run Your Business. Connect Everything.**
>
> One ecosystem. Two products. OrbitanOS runs your workforce and operations. Orbit Nexus powers it with AI.

## Target Segments

1. **Home-Based Businesses** — Free plan, lightweight workflows
2. **Startups & SMEs** — Starter/Growth plans, single outlet
3. **Multi-Outlet Businesses** — Growth/Business plans, multiple outlets
4. **Multi-Brand Organisations** — Business/Enterprise plans, multiple brands
5. **Enterprise** — Enterprise plan, dedicated infrastructure

## Launch Plan

1. **Soft Launch** — Pilot tenants validate MVP (July 2026)
2. **Public Launch** — Landing page live, self-serve onboarding (Q3 2026)
3. **Enterprise Launch** — Compliance certifications, direct sales (2027)
