# Future Research Targets

## Purpose

Lists competitors and reference companies planned for future research. Each will get its own file in the Research library when research is conducted.

## Planned Research

### Enterprise ERP
- **SAP** — Enterprise ERP giant, business suite, industry solutions
- **Oracle** — Enterprise ERP, database, cloud applications, NetSuite for SMB

### SMB / Mid-Market
- **Zoho** — Business operating system, multi-product suite for SMBs
- **HubSpot** — CRM, marketing, sales, service platform

### HR / Workforce
- **BambooHR** — SMB HR management
- **Gusto** — SMB payroll and benefits
- **Deel** — Global hiring and payroll

### Project / Work Management
- **Asana** — Work management platform
- **ClickUp** — Productivity platform
- **Notion** — All-in-one workspace

### Finance / Accounting
- **Xero** — SMB accounting (Orbitan integration partner, also a competitor for financial workflows)
- **QuickBooks** — SMB accounting

### E-commerce
- **WooCommerce** — WordPress e-commerce plugin
- **BigCommerce** — E-commerce platform

### Identity / Security
- **Okta** — Enterprise identity and SSO
- **Auth0** — Identity platform for developers
- **Veza** — Access governance platform

### Industry-Specific
- **Toast** — Restaurant management platform (F&B competitor)
- **Square** — POS and payment processing (retail/restaurant)
- **Lightspeed** — POS and e-commerce (retail/restaurant)
